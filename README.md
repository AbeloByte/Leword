## The problem

You're watching a show. Someone says a word you've never heard. You think *"nice word, I should remember that"* — and by the next scene, it's gone.

Leword is where it goes instead.

## How it works

**1. Catch it.** Save the word the moment you hear it, along with the line it appeared in and the movie, show, or book it came from. Context is what makes a word stick.

**2. Keep it.** Everything you save lands in your own word bank — organized by category, searchable, and yours alone.

**3. Master it.** A new word waits for you every day. Flashcards drill the ones you haven't nailed yet. Tap any word to hear it said out loud.

**4. Keep showing up.** Your streak counts every day you add something. Watch your mastered count climb.

## What's inside

🗂️ **Your word bank** — search it, sort it by category, tick words off as mastered

📅 **Word of the Day** — one word from your own collection, picked fresh each morning

🃏 **Flashcards** — flip to reveal, drill what hasn't stuck

🔥 **Streaks & stats** — days in a row, words collected, mastery rate

🔊 **Say it out loud** — hear any word pronounced, with a parrot who talks along

🌓 **Yours, everywhere** — installs like an app on phone or desktop, light or dark
